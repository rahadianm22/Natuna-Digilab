and /tabs at 1440, 1024, 768 and 390px in light and dark mode, plus /docs, /foundation, /themes, /components/link,

the clipboard, rendered it on a blank page, and compared it with the live preview. I measured the hero callouts against
their targets with and without reduced motion. I read the source behind every finding.

## Findings

### 1. HIGH. In dark mode, hovering the destructive Button turns it into white text on pale pink
- Where: every destructive Button in dark mode, for example the Delete button on /components/button and on Home
  (`src/ui/button.tsx:16`).
- What: `destructive: "bg-danger text-white hover:bg-red-800"`. The dark theme flips `--color-red-800` to `#f7b6b7`
  (`globals.css:199`), so on hover the button measures `rgb(247,182,183)` with white text, about 1.7:1. In the
  screenshot, the label nearly disappears. The same pale pink shows while the button is loading, because the pointer is
  still over it after the click. `bg-danger` does not flip, but its hover step does. Primary avoids this problem
  because it uses the fixed `brand-hover` token.
- Why: the destructive action is the one button that has to stay readable, and the axe tests miss it because they
  never hover.
- Fix: add `--color-danger-hover: #8c2b2c` to `@theme`, next to `--color-brand-hover`. Leave it out of the dark block,
  and use `hover:bg-danger-hover`. Add one Playwright check that hovers each variant in dark mode and asserts 4.5:1.

### 2. HIGH. The hero callouts sit 15px below their parts, and in Light screen mode the leaders disappear
- Where: Home hero at 1280px and wider (`src/components/home/HeroPhone.tsx:45-64`, `114-115`, `122`;
  `globals.css:256-260`, `347-371`).
- What:
  - Offset: `measure()` runs in `useLayoutEffect` on mount. At that moment the device has the `rise` animation with
    `both` fill, so it is still at `translateY(14px)`. After the animation, nothing changes size, so the
    ResizeObserver never fires again. Measured at 1440: title center at y=232 and its callout at 246; card at 286 and
    301; Send at 597 and 612. With reduced motion, all four match exactly. Typing an error, which changes the height,
    also snaps them into place. "Send another" re-measures during `screen-in`, so the same 14px error comes back.
  - Light screen mode: the leader is `bg-white/80` and the dot has a `border-white` ring. Over the white screen, the
    inner part of each leader disappears, so the lines look as if they stop at the device edge again.
  - Bezel: `.theme-light, .theme-dark { background: var(--color-canvas) }` is unlayered, so it beats the
    `bg-[#0d121c]` utility on the device. The computed device background equals the screen (`#19212e` dark, `#ffffff`
    light). The phone has no bezel, which leaves "shadow-xl" pointing at a white slab in Light mode.
- Why: the comment says "the picture is also documentation". Labels that are 15px off look careless in the first
  picture visitors see, and that picture is what audit 003 #5 set out to fix.
- Fix:
  - Measure with the transform removed: read `offsetTop`/`offsetLeft` relative to `wrap` (layout boxes ignore
    transforms) instead of `getBoundingClientRect`. Alternatively, re-run `measure` on the device's `animationend`
    and on the form's `animationend`.
  - Draw leaders and dots in a color that reads on both screens and on the blue panel: `bg-[#0d121c]` with a
    `ring-1 ring-white` dot, or stop the line at the bezel and put the dot on the part with
    `ring-2 ring-[#026acc]`.
  - Move the background in `globals.css:256-260` into `@layer base`, so utilities win, or set the device color with
    `!bg-[#0d121c]`.

### 3. HIGH. Previews and demos break the rules printed under them
- Where: /components/button (`demos.tsx:20`, `component-docs.ts:37,57`), /modal (`ComponentPreview.tsx:185-196`),
  /alert (`:202-208`), /otp-input (`:397-413`), /link (`:78-82`). Rules are in `components-data.ts:256,278,467-469,91`.
- What: audit 003 #3 fixed this pattern for QR Code. These pages still show it:
  - Button: the only live demo leads with a primary button labeled "Clicked 0". The Do column beside it says "Write
    labels that describe the action", and the code people copy carries the same label. Large, loading and disabled
    appear only on the overview thumbnail, never on the page itself.
  - Modal: Do says "Provide a clear way to dismiss (close button, escape key, backdrop click)". The preview has no
    close button and no backdrop. Its Cancel and Delete are hand-rolled `<button>`s, 30px tall and 12px text, which
    does not match the system's own Button (40/44px, 14px). They are also focusable on the page and do nothing.
  - Alert: Do says "Match color and icon to severity". The preview has no icon, and its 12px text is smaller than any
    body style.
  - OTP Input: usage says "Accept a pasted code and move focus between boxes automatically", and Don't says "Block
    paste". The preview inputs are live. Pasting "482913" into the first box leaves "4|||||", and typing does not
    move focus. Do asks for "where the code was sent and a way to resend it", but the label says only "Code sent to
    your phone" and there is no resend.
  - Link: Do is about inline links in body text, but the preview is a lone underlined line inside a `div`, with no
    sentence around it.
- Why: on a design-system page, the picture is the rule people copy. When the picture breaks the text, readers
  follow the picture.
- Fix:
  - Button demo: replace the counter with real actions, such as `Save changes` (primary), `Cancel` (ghost), and
    `Delete account` (destructive, loading on click), then a second row with `size="lg"` and `disabled`. Update both
    usage snippets.
  - Modal: render a `bg-ink/40` scrim behind the dialog, add a 44px close `X` (`aria-label="Close"`), use
    `<Button variant="ghost">` and `<Button variant="destructive">` (or `buttonStyles` on spans so they are inert),
    and use payment copy ("Cancel this transfer?").
  - Alert: add a `Warning` icon (`size={18}`) and set the text to `text-sm`.
  - OTP: make the preview inert (`readOnly tabIndex={-1}` on spans styled as boxes) and show a filled state
    (`4 8 2 _ _ _`), "Sent to +62 812 •••• 4821", and a "Resend in 0:45" link. If it stays live, handle `onPaste`
    and auto-advance.
  - Link: show it in a sentence: "Read the <a>transfer limits</a> before you send."

### 4. MEDIUM. Copy to Figma: lost underlines, a spinner 40% too large, and layer names that read badly
- Where: `src/lib/dom-to-svg.ts:58-93`, `136-173`, `227-234`, `292-293`. Seen on /link, /spinner, /button (loading),
  /drawer and every export.
- What (each checked in the rendered SVG):
  - Underline: the /link preview is underlined, but the export is plain blue text. `textRuns` never reads
    `textDecorationLine`.
  - Spinning ring: `box()` sizes the ring from `getBoundingClientRect()`, which for a rotating element is the rotated
    bounding box. The /spinner preview is `h-6 w-6` (24px), but the export draws arcs with `r=15.76`, about a 33px
    ring. The loading Button spinner (16px) came out with `r=7.8` instead of 7. The size changes with each copy.
  - Clipping: the Drawer scrim exports as a square `rect` over a container with `rx=7.5`. `overflow:hidden` is
    applied as a box clip only, so the scrim corners stick out of the rounded frame.
  - Layer names: text layers repeat their parent's name, and numbering is global across the file, so Figma shows
    "Pending > Pending 2", "Pay now > Pay now 2", "2 > 2 2" (Stepper), "Bills 2", "Background 7" (OTP), and three
    "Natuna Digilab" avatars. Names are cut mid-word ("Most transfers arrive within a m", "List / Identity 2 Selfie 3
    Revi", "How long does a transfer"). Bordered boxes with no role are still "Frame" (Accordion, Card, Modal,
    Stepper circles).
- Why: home promises "editable layers". A designer who pastes an unlined link, an oversized spinner, and "2 2" in
  the layer list has to clean up by hand, which is the work the button exists to save.
- Fix:
  - In `textRuns`, when `cs.textDecorationLine` includes `underline`, add `text-decoration="underline"` (Figma
    imports it), or emit a 1px `<line>` at `baseline + size*0.12` in the text color.
  - In `box()`, take `w` and `h` from `el.offsetWidth` and `offsetHeight` (or `cs.width`), centered on the rect
    center, the same way `icon()` already does.
  - When a clipping ancestor has a radius, wrap its children in `<clipPath><rect rx=…/></clipPath>`.
  - Number names per parent, not per file (reset the counter for each `<g>`). Do not create a group for a box whose
    only child is its own text; name it "Label" instead. Cut names at a word boundary and add an ellipsis
    (`text.split(" ")` up to 32 characters, then `…`). Name a bordered box with no role "Container", or use
    `data-figma-name` on Card, Modal and the Stepper step (`ComponentPreview.tsx:108,187,498`).

### 5. MEDIUM. The Button loading prop claims a fixed width, but the button grows 49%
- Where: /components/button, Props table (`component-docs.ts:70`), `button.tsx:32,45-47`, `demos.tsx:24`.
- What: the description says "The label stays so the width does not change". Clicking Delete grows the button from
  72px to 107px: the spinner adds 16px plus the 8px gap, and the demo also changes the label to "Deleting". Every
  button beside it shifts.
- Why: the doc makes a promise that the live demo above it breaks, and moving controls during a destructive action
  invites a misclick.
- Fix: keep the label and overlay the spinner. Make the button `relative`, wrap the children in
  `<span className={loading ? "invisible" : ""}>`, and center the spinner with `absolute inset-0 m-auto`. Keep "Delete"
  as the label while loading (`aria-busy` already announces the state), or keep the doc and stop claiming a fixed
  width.

### 6. MEDIUM. Five components with pages are missing from the Components catalog
- Where: /components (`ComponentOverview.tsx:148`, which renders only `trackerGroups`), and the breadcrumb on
  untracked pages (`[slug]/page.tsx:78-80`).
- What: the sidebar lists a "Not tracked" group with 5 components (Button Group, Code Block, Icon Button, Link,
  Select), and each has a page. The catalog says "All 51" and shows only Atoms (19) and Molecules (32), so those five
  never appear. On /components/link, the Related section's "See all components" link leads to a page that does not
  list Link, and the breadcrumb "Not tracked" is plain text with nowhere to go.
- Why: the catalog is the index. A page you can reach only through the sidebar or Ctrl K looks like an accident.
- Fix: add a third section, "Not tracked", after Molecules, built from `components.filter(c => c.status ===
  "untracked")`, with the dashed badge and the line "Pages without a tracker row: no sign-off and no code yet." Give
  it a filter chip, and link the breadcrumb to `/components#group-Not tracked`.

### 7. MEDIUM. Catalog names mix slashes, plurals and parentheses, and the sidebar truncates them
- Where: /components cards and the sidebar (`components-data.ts:341,385,429,517,528,561,583`, which mirror
  `natuna-tracker.ts`). Category for Link: `components-data.ts:86`.
- What: "Chip / Tag", "Table / Data Table", "Combobox / Autocomplete", "Amount / Currency Input", "Navigation Bar
  (Top/Bottom)" and "Progress Bars" (plural) sit beside singular, single names. The sidebar cuts three of them to
  "Amount / Currency In…", "Combobox / Autocom…" and "Navigation Bar (Top/B…". "Radio Button" (Atoms) and "Radio"
  (Molecules, a fieldset of options) read as duplicates. Link's category is "Action", so its page says "Category
  Action" and Related says "other action components", while its own usage says "Use for navigation".
- Why: these names become Figma component names and code exports. Aliases written with slashes are Notion
  shorthand, not names.
- Fix: one name per component, with the alias in `tags` so search still finds it: Chip, Table, Combobox, Amount
  Input, Navigation Bar, Progress Bar, Radio Group (slug is already `radio-group`). Keep the Notion names in the
  tracker `name` only. Change Link's category to Navigation.

### 8. MEDIUM. The focus ring disappears on the blue hero panel
- Where: Home, "Screen mode" Light and Dark buttons (`HeroPhone.tsx:221-234`), with the global ring in
  `globals.css:275-278`. Also the Amount Input preview (`ComponentPreview.tsx:477`).
- What: tabbing to the selected "Dark" button gives a 2px outline measured at `rgb(1,80,153)`, on a `#026acc` panel
  next to a `#013566` track. That is about 1.5:1, and in the capture the ring cannot be seen. The Amount Input preview
  sets `outline-none` and shows focus only as a 1px border color change.
- Why: these are the only interactive controls in the hero, and a keyboard user loses their place there. WCAG 2.4.13
  and 1.4.11 ask for 3:1.
- Fix: on the panel controls, add `focus-visible:outline-white` (white on `#026acc` is 5:1). On the amount field, use
  the Input pattern: `focus-within:ring-2 focus-within:ring-blue-600 focus-within:ring-offset-1`.

### 9. LOW. The Foundation section row does not show which section you are in
